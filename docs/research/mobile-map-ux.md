# Mobile map UX research

Ticket: `.scratch/house-cost-here-mvp/issues/01-mobile-map-ux-research.md`. Written 2026-09-17 against the pinned versions in `.scratch/house-cost-here-mvp/versions.md` (maplibre-gl 6.10.0, lighthouse 13.4.1, @lhci/cli 0.15.1, nuxt 4.5.2).

Every number below cites the source that owns it. "Measured" means it was checked directly on 2026-09-17 (bundle downloaded and gzipped, style JSON parsed, Overpass queried); the command lines are in the evidence sections so the measurement can be repeated.

## Decisions

### 1. Search Radius

| Decision | Value | Why (source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radius options | `[250, 500, 1000]` m | Each step is exactly one zoom level on a phone (OSM zoom maths, [wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Zoom_levels](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Zoom_levels)); 2000 m is dropped, see Overturned defaults |
| Default | `500` m | Between Walk Score's "5 minute walk (.25 miles)" full-credit distance ([walkscore.com/methodology](https://www.walkscore.com/methodology.shtml)) and TfL's 640 m / 8 min walk-to-bus assumption ([content.tfl.gov.uk/connectivity-assessment-guide.pdf](https://content.tfl.gov.uk/connectivity-assessment-guide.pdf)); ~2,000 OSM buildings in a dense city centre, ~300 in a village (measured, Overpass) |
| Map zoom per radius (412 px wide phone, lat 52°) | 250 m → z16, 500 m → z15, 1000 m → z14; the circle is 340 px wide at each | Derived from the OSM formula `C·cos(lat)/2^(z+8)` |

| Side panel width | one third of the window | Supporting pane: primary area "typically about two thirds" ([canonical-layouts](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/layouts/adaptive/canonical-layouts)) |

### 3. Map controls

| Decision | Value | Why (source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hit target | `48 × 48` CSS px, `8px` gap | Android: "at least 48x48dp, separated by 8dp of space or more" ([support.google.com … 7101858](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858)); Apple: hit region "at least 44x44 pt" ([HIG Buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons)); WCAG 2.5.5 (AAA) 44 × 44, 2.5.8 (AA) 24 × 24 ([w3.org … target-size-enhanced](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-enhanced.html), [target-size-minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)); NN/g 1 cm × 1 cm ([nngroup.com/articles/touch-target-size](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/touch-target-size/)). 48 satisfies all four |
| MapLibre default button | `29 × 29 px` — must be overridden | Measured in `maplibre-gl@6.10.0/dist/maplibre-gl.css`: `.maplibregl-ctrl-group button{width:29px;height:29px}` ([unpkg.com/maplibre-gl@6.10.0/dist/maplibre-gl.css](https://unpkg.com/maplibre-gl@6.10.0/dist/maplibre-gl.css)) |
| Placement (phone) | right edge, stacked, bottom of stack at `calc(peek + 16px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`; nothing in the four corners; search box at top under `env(safe-area-inset-top)` | Corner targets need ~12 mm vs 7 mm at centre; "Place secondary actions along the top and bottom edges", tertiary in corners ([Hoober 2017](https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2017/03/design-for-fingers-touch-and-people-part-1.php)); 49 % one-handed, 36 % cradled, 15 % two-handed, right thumb 67 % of one-handed use ([Hoober 2013](https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2013/02/how-do-users-really-hold-mobile-devices.php)); safe areas via `viewport-fit=cover` and `env(safe-area-inset-*)` ([webkit.org/blog/7929](https://webkit.org/blog/7929/designing-websites-for-iphone-x/)) |
| Which controls on a phone | locate-me (`GeolocateControl`), radius chooser, "search here" (appears after a drag); no zoom buttons, no compass unless the map is rotated | "People expect to be able to zoom, pan, and otherwise interact with maps in familiar ways" ([HIG Maps](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps)); pinch zoom is on by default ([MapLibre MapOptions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/type-aliases/MapOptions/)) |
| Which controls at `>= 840px` | add `NavigationControl` (zoom ± and compass) | Keyboard/mouse users have no pinch ([NavigationControlOptions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/type-aliases/NavigationControlOptions/)) |
| Gestures | `touchZoomRotate: true`, `touchPitch: false`, `pitchWithRotate: false`, `cooperativeGestures: false`, `maxPitch: 0` | Defaults and meanings from [MapOptions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/type-aliases/MapOptions/); pitch is never useful for this app and two-finger drags otherwise tilt the map by accident |
| Attribution | `AttributionControl({ compact: true })` bottom-left, kept above the sheet's peek so it is never covered | Default is responsive below 640 px ([AttributionControlOptions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/type-aliases/AttributionControlOptions/)); attribution is required by both tile hosts ([openfreemap.org](https://openfreemap.org/), VersaTiles `tiles.json` `attribution` field, measured) |
| Contrast | controls on a solid surface with a shadow | "Make sure there's enough contrast between custom controls and the map" ([HIG Maps](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps)) |

### 4. Markers

| Decision | Value | Why (source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rendering | one GeoJSON source with `cluster: true`, drawn with `symbol`/`circle` layers; no HTML `Marker` instances except the user-location dot | "adding hundreds of Markers to a Mapbox GL Map can cause a web browser to become unresponsive or sluggish… displaying the same dataset of hundreds of points in a circle or symbol layer is not likely to cause a noticeable performance impact" ([docs.mapbox.com … markers-vs-layers](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/troubleshooting/markers-vs-layers/)); MapLibre `Marker` is an HTML element ([MapLibre Marker](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/classes/Marker/)) |
| Price-labelled vs plain | price pill (label + fill) when a Price Signal exists; plain outlined circle otherwise; selected = larger pill, distinct colour, raised `symbol-sort-key` | "Clearly identify elements that people select… use distinct styling like an outline and color variation" ([HIG Maps](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps)) |
| Label text | `Intl.NumberFormat(locale, { style: 'currency', currency, notation: 'compact', maximumFractionDigits: 1 })`, max 7 characters, one line | `notation: 'compact'` / `compactDisplay: 'short'` ([MDN NumberFormat](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Intl/NumberFormat/NumberFormat)); measured in Node 24: `£285k`, `£1.3m`, `€ 450K`, `€ 1,3 mln.`, `$1M`, `￥4500万` |
| Cluster radius | `clusterRadius: 56` | Default is 50 px ([MapLibre style spec, GeoJSON source](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-style-spec/sources/)); 56 = 48 px target + 8 px gap so two visible markers never share a thumb-sized area; NN/g: densely placed pins "cannot be accurately selected, because a user's finger pad is too large" ([nngroup.com/articles/mobile-maps-locations](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-maps-locations/)) |
| Cluster max zoom | `clusterMaxZoom: 16` (clusters shown up to z16, individual houses from z17) | "setting clusterMaxZoom to 14 means the clusters will be displayed until z15" ([style spec](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-style-spec/sources/)); at z17 and lat 52°, 56 px ≈ 41 m, so most houses separate; `clusterMinPoints` stays at the default 2 |
| Overlap at z17+ | leave `text-allow-overlap`/`icon-allow-overlap` false so colliding labels are hidden rather than stacked; hidden houses stay reachable from the list | MapLibre collision behaviour is the layout default; NN/g finding above |
| Cluster marker | 40 px circle with count, hit-tested with a 48 px box | Apple: clusters "expand to progressively reveal individual points of interest" as people zoom ([HIG Maps](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps)) |
| Tap handling | `map.queryRenderedFeatures([[x-24, y-24], [x+24, y+24]], { layers })` so the hit area is 48 px even when the pill is smaller | [Map#queryRenderedFeatures](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/classes/Map/#queryrenderedfeatures) accepts a bounding box |

### 5. Reduced motion and dark mode

| Decision | Value | Why (source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reduced motion, camera | leave `reduceMotion` unset; never pass `essential: true`; use `easeTo` with `duration <= 300` for selection, `fitBounds` for radius changes | `reduceMotion`: "If not provided, gesture inertia defaults to the user's device settings" ([MapOptions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/type-aliases/MapOptions/)); `easeTo`/`flyTo` "will happen instantly if the user has enabled the reduced motion accessibility feature… unless options includes essential: true" ([Map](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/classes/Map/), [AnimationOptions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/type-aliases/AnimationOptions/)); measured: 6.10.0 reads `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` |
| Reduced motion, labels | `fadeDuration: 0` when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` | Default 300 ms label fade ([MapOptions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/type-aliases/MapOptions/)); Apple: reduce "zooming, scaling, and peripheral motion", replace movement with fades ([HIG Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility), [HIG Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion)) |
| Reduced motion, UI | sheet snap transitions drop to `0ms` (position jumps, content cross-fades) under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` | [MDN prefers-reduced-motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion) |
| Dark mode detection | `matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: dark)')`, follow the OS, plus Nuxt UI's colour mode | [MDN prefers-color-scheme](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-color-scheme); Apple: people "generally expect all apps and games to respect their preference" ([HIG Dark Mode](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode)) |
| Style switching | `map.setStyle(url, { transformStyle })` keeping the default diff; re-add the app's sources/layers in `transformStyle` | "If false, force a 'full' update" so diff is the default; `transformStyle` modifies the fetched style before commit ([StyleSwapOptions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/type-aliases/StyleSwapOptions/)); measured: VersaTiles `colorful` and `eclipse` have identical layer ids (324 each) so the diff path applies, OpenFreeMap `liberty` (111 layers) vs `dark` (47) share 5 ids so a switch is a full rebuild |
| Marker colours | separate light/dark palette; markers use `['case', …]` on a `dark` feature-state or are re-styled on switch | Apple: Dark Mode uses "dimmer background colors and brighter foreground colors" ([HIG Dark Mode](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode)) |

### 7. Loading pattern

| Decision | Value | Why (source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order | SSR shell (list + facts) → hydrate → Location known → `import('maplibre-gl')` → `map.on('load')` → swap placeholder for canvas | User stories 21–22; Lighthouse flags files with > 20 KiB unused JS and recommends route-level lazy loading ([unused-javascript](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/performance/unused-javascript)) |
| Placeholder | `<ClientOnly>` with a `#fallback` slot rendered on the server: a fixed-height (`100dvh` minus peek) neutral panel with a faint grid and the text summary of the Search Area | `#fallback` "content to be rendered on the server and displayed until `<ClientOnly>` is mounted" ([nuxt.com … client-only](https://nuxt.com/docs/4.x/api/components/client-only)); fixed height keeps CLS ≤ 0.1 |
| Progress indicator | none for the first 1 s; an indeterminate spinner on the placeholder from 1 s; a percent/step indicator ("loading map 2/3") if a load is still running at 10 s | 0.1 s / 1 s / 10 s limits ([nngroup.com … response-times](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/response-times-3-important-limits/)); spinners "best used when the page takes 2–10 seconds to load" and on "a single module, like a video or a card", skeletons for whole-page loads under 10 s ([nngroup.com/articles/skeleton-screens](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/skeleton-screens/)) |
| Failure | `error` state shows "Map failed to load" with a retry button on the placeholder; the list and facts keep working | Spec user story 41; a11y story 44 |
| Feedback during interaction | keep the spinner visible while the map is `loading` after pan/zoom ("search here" flow) | NN/g: on slow connections users "zoomed or panned farther than intended due to repeating gestures multiple times, in the absence of any feedback" ([mobile-maps-locations](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-maps-locations/)) |

## Overturned defaults

Each of these changes a value the spec or `00-README.md` states; ticket 14 records the reasoning in an ADR.

1. **Radius options `[250, 500, 1000, 2000]` → `[250, 500, 1000]`.** Building counts grow with r²: measured on 2026-09-17 via Overpass (`way|relation[building]` around the point), Amsterdam centre gives 540 / 2,064 / 8,588 / 27,778 buildings at 250 / 500 / 1000 / 2000 m, London Islington 511 / 2,258 / 6,263 / 18,524, suburban Amstelveen 545 / 2,131 / 10,215 / 24,186, the village of Chipping Campden 178 / 313 / 456 / 585. A 500 m `out tags center` response measured 730,461 B raw (52,607 B gzip), about 354 B per building, so 2000 m in a city is ~10 MB per uncached query against a public instance whose policy is "download less than 1 GB data per day" and "less than 10,000 queries per day" ([wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API)). At 2000 m the map sits at z13, and the VersaTiles `buildings` layer only exists at z14 (`tiles.json` `vector_layers` minzoom 14, measured), so no footprints would be drawn under the markers. 2000 m is also a 25 min walk, past TfL's 960 m rail-walk limit and near Walk Score's 30 min cut-off. 1000 m already exceeds any plausible house cap in every urban sample, so the cap message will do the honest work there.
2. **The bottom sheet hosts the house list and Neighbourhood Facts, not only the selected-house card.** The spec puts the list "beneath the map" on a scrolling page, but a full-viewport map has no gutter to start a page scroll from: "users commonly begin their swiping gesture on the lower half of the screen… this gesture ends up panning the map content in place instead of scrolling the page" ([nngroup.com/articles/mobile-maps-locations](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-maps-locations/)). NN/g's own model of a non-modal sheet is Google Maps, where the sheet "shows details about a destination… while still allowing the user to pan and zoom" ([bottom-sheet](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/bottom-sheet/)). So: map fixed at `100dvh`, sheet at peek shows the count summary ("42 houses, 18 with prices"), half shows the list, full shows list then facts; selecting a house pushes the card into the sheet with Back returning to the list. The DOM order stays map-summary-list-facts for SSR and screen readers, which is what user stories 23 and 44 need. Note the tension NN/g found for store locators (users preferred a list first); the sheet at half height keeps the list one drag away without giving up the map-first promise.
3. **Control size: MapLibre's 29 px buttons → 48 px.** See decision 3; the default CSS fails Apple 44 pt, Android 48 dp and WCAG 2.5.5.
4. **"Interactive within a couple of seconds" is enforced as LCP ≤ 2.5 s + TBT ≤ 200 ms, not TTI.** TTI is unscored and hidden since Lighthouse 10 ([interactive](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/performance/interactive)); it stays as a warn-only assertion at 3.8 s.
5. **MapLibre is lazy-loaded with a dynamic `import()`, not through Nuxt Scripts.** `useScript` injects a script URL and its `bundle` option copies a remote script to the origin ([scripts.nuxt.com … use-script](https://scripts.nuxt.com/docs/api/use-script)), which suits third-party tags. maplibre-gl 6.10.0 ships only ESM files (`dist/maplibre-gl.mjs`, no UMD `dist/maplibre-gl.js`; the jsDelivr file listing at [data.jsdelivr.com/v1/package/npm/maplibre-gl@6.10.0/flat](https://data.jsdelivr.com/v1/package/npm/maplibre-gl@6.10.0/flat) and a 404 for the `.js` path were both checked), so the idiomatic path is a `<ClientOnly>` component that does `await import('maplibre-gl')` after Location is known; Vite emits it as its own chunk and nothing lands in the entry bundle. Nuxt Scripts stays in the module list only if a CDN copy is ever wanted.

Confirmed as stated in the spec: three snap points; side panel on wide screens; MapLibre with vector tiles; VersaTiles as host with the two style URLs; built-in GeoJSON clustering; compact currency labels; default radius 500 m.

## Evidence

### Radius

- Walk Score: "Amenities within a 5 minute walk (.25 miles) are given maximum points… with no points given after a 30 minute walk." [walkscore.com/methodology.shtml](https://www.walkscore.com/methodology.shtml)
- TfL connectivity guide: "The calculation assumes that people will walk up to 640 metres (approximately eight minutes) to a bus service and up to 960 metres (12 minutes) to a rail or Tube service." [content.tfl.gov.uk/connectivity-assessment-guide.pdf](https://content.tfl.gov.uk/connectivity-assessment-guide.pdf) (page text extracted locally). 640 m / 8 min = 80 m/min.
- OSM zoom maths: metres per pixel = `C · cos(lat) / 2^(z+8)`, `C = 40,075,016.686 m`; at the equator z13 19.109, z14 9.555, z15 4.777, z16 2.389, z17 1.194 m/px. [wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Zoom_levels](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Zoom_levels). At lat 52°: z13 11.76, z14 5.88, z15 2.94, z16 1.47, z17 0.735 m/px. A 1000 m diameter at z15 is 340 px; the Lighthouse phone is 412 px wide. The same 340 px holds for 500 m at z16 and 2000 m at z14, which is why a ×2 progression feels consistent: every radius step is one zoom level.
- Overpass building counts (2026-09-17, `overpass-api.de`, query `(way[building][building!=no](around:R,lat,lon);relation[building][building!=no](around:R,lat,lon););out count;`):

  | Place | 250 m | 500 m | 1000 m | 2000 m |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Amsterdam centre 52.3702, 4.8952 | 540 | 2,064 | 8,588 | 27,778 |
